## Research Goal

The goal was to understand how customers experienced the service and continuously bring the voice of the customer into product conversations.

---

## Research / Measurement Framework

Show the relationship between:

**Customer Effort Score (CES)**  
+  
**Customer Value / Value Assessment (CVA)**  
+  
**CSAT**  
+  
**In-context communication feedback**  


![Picture of Report](assets/images/CESCVA.png)
<figcaption class="caption">CES CVA SURVEY</figcaption>

The value came from combining multiple signals to understand both **what customers experienced** and **why**.

---

## What I Built

I personally:

- Built the data query
- Built the automation workflow
- Created a daily digest
- Sent customer feedback into the team's shared inbox

### Measurement Workflow

**Customer feedback**  
→  
**Query**  
→  
**Automated workflow**  
→  
**Daily digest**  
→  
**Team discussion**  
→  
**Product action**

---

## Impact

The program generated high engagement and sparked product strategy conversations.

### The Unexpected Insight

Customer feedback included **billing-related issues**.

This revealed that the Service Health team was receiving feedback about a customer problem that involved another organization.

This led to:

**Feedback**  
→  
**Unexpected problem**  
→  
**Identify missing stakeholder**  
→  
**Engage billing team**

### Key Customer Problem

**Impacted resources emerged as the #1 customer problem.**

---

## Strategic Impact

The measurement program helped shift the team's thinking from:

> **"What did customers say?"**

to:

> **"What customer problems should influence our product vision?"**

The program connected customer feedback, UX measurement, and behavioral data to broader product strategy.

---

## What Changed Because of the Program?

The value of the measurement program was not the metrics themselves.

It was creating a **continuous mechanism for customer evidence to influence product decisions**.

**Customer evidence → Measurement → Insight → Product strategy → Action**
