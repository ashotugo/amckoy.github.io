## Context

Microsoft/Azure invests approximately **$34M in on-call engineering (OCE) effort**.

The research question was:

> **Where are engineers spending their time during incident response, and where are the biggest opportunities to reduce operational burden?**

Use the **$34M** figure prominently as a contextual metric.

---

## Research Strategy

Rather than studying incident response abstractly, I focused the research on:

- Core Azure services
- Services experiencing the most outages
- A specific service, including SQL Database
- Engineers with recent live-site/on-call experience
- A single real incident

### Why Focus on a Real Incident?

Rather than asking engineers to describe their entire incident-response experience from memory, I used a recent live incident to reconstruct the end-to-end journey.

> **A real incident provided a concrete anchor for understanding where time, effort, and coordination were required.**

## Key Insight

The research identified **where the greatest amount of engineering time was being spent during the incident journey**.

Make this the centerpiece of the case study.

### From Time to Opportunity

**Where engineers spend time**  
+  
**Why they spend time there**  
+  
**What organizational / product changes could reduce that burden**

This moved the conversation beyond simply documenting pain points toward identifying opportunities to reduce operational burden.

---

## Cross-Org Action

One important outcome was assigning PMs across the organization to address specific pain points.

Show this visually:

**Pain point**  
→  
**Owning team**  
→  
**PM**  
→  
**Product opportunity**

The research exposed problems that could not be solved by one team alone.

The research therefore became a **mechanism for coordinating work across the organization**.

---

## Strategic Takeaway

> **The research turned a complex, distributed incident-response experience into a concrete set of opportunities with owners across the organization.**

---

## Research Impact

- Identified where engineering time was concentrated
- Created an end-to-end view of the DRI journey
- Grounded findings in a real incident
- Identified actionable pain points
- Connected pain points to PM owners
- Highlighted opportunities requiring cross-org collaboration
- Connected UX research to a significant operational cost
